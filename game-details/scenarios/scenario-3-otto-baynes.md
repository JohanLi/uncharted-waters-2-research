# Scenario 3: Otto Baynes

Otto's story uses Piracy Fame, Palace and Guild assignments, and tightly staged
before/after-battle hooks. Its 20,000-Fame gold-frigate rumor is optional; the
main story can advance without ever showing it.

## Story and threshold map

| Section |    Piracy Fame gate | Story                                       |
| ------: | ------------------: | ------------------------------------------- |
|       0 |                   — | Royal commission and stolen Spanish Galleon |
|       1 |                   — | Spanish counterattack                       |
|       2 |         5,000 twice | Ghost rumors and Catalina                   |
|       3 |              20,000 | Optional Pietro gold-frigate rumor          |
|       4 |              30,000 | Spanish Armada campaign                     |
|       5 | 30,000 continuation | Ezequiel, Bordeaux appointment, and ending  |

## Section guide

### 0–1: commission and Spanish pursuit

London and Seville routes stage Henry VIII's commission, Matthew's duel, the
ship theft, a midnight reward, and the first Spanish attack. Section 1 then
uses before/after-battle hooks to finish the counterattack. It has no new Fame
threshold.

### 2: ghosts and Catalina

Two separate 5,000 Piracy Fame comparisons gate the Trader encounter and the
later Pub meeting. Completing one does not imply that the other building stage
has already been armed.

### 3: optional gold frigate

At 20,000 Piracy Fame, Pietro can describe a Spanish frigate carrying gold out
of Veracruz. Runtime observation confirms that this scene is fully optional,
skippable, and easy to miss. It is not a prerequisite for the Armada campaign.

### 4: Armada campaign

The 30,000-Fame section contains the London summons, Guild intelligence,
Nantes battle, South American report, Santo Domingo, the Amazon, and
Catalina's intervention.

### 5: Ezequiel and London ending

A second 30,000 comparison guards the continuation: a Pub meeting with
Ezequiel, a 30-day Bordeaux appointment, battle hooks, and the return to
London. `EB 00 00 05` creates a five-way random branch in this phase.

## Practical progression guide

| If the story appears stuck at… | Check…                                                                   |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Opening commission             | Follow London and Seville building prompts before seeking another battle |
| Spanish pursuit                | The encounter must occur in the expected before/after-battle subsection  |
| 5,000 Fame                     | Complete both the Trader and later Pub stages                            |
| 20,000 Fame                    | The Pietro rumor is optional; continue raising Fame if it was missed     |
| 30,000 Fame                    | Return to London, then follow Guild/Nantes/South America prompts         |
| Bordeaux appointment           | The continuation includes a 30-day wait and another 30,000 check         |

## Highest-value validation

- Controlled captures around the two 5,000-Fame building stages.
- Exact trigger building and availability window for the optional 20,000 scene.
- The five possible outcomes selected by `EB 00 00 05`.
